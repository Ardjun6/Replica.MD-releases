Your data.
Clear boundaries.

ReplicaMD is a local-first desktop app. “Local-first” describes where workspace data lives; it does not mean every feature runs offline.

On your computer

Profiles, workspace state, local databases, agent worktrees and memory, and Knowledge content are stored on your computer. The usual app-data home is ~/.replicamd; development profiles or configured homes can use another directory. Local files and exports can contain sensitive information. Protect your device and backups.

Your AI provider

When you run an AI agent, the installed CLI may send prompts, code, files, and other context to its configured provider. Its authentication, retention, privacy terms, and costs apply. Review your CLI and provider settings before sharing private repositories. ReplicaMD does not make provider requests private merely by organizing them locally.

Optional Google connections

Local profiles work without Google. Optional account linking requests OpenID, email, and profile information to identify the connected account; this does not grant access to your Gmail inbox.

Connecting Google Calendar separately requests read access to your calendar list and access to calendar events. This allows calendar loading and event operations, including creation and deletion. Authorization and calendar requests go to Google. Connection tokens are retained locally for continued access. Disconnect in the app and revoke access in your Google account when you no longer need it.

Google is currently in Testing and restricted to approved test users. It is not generally available to every visitor.

Analytics and crash reporting

In the inspected desktop source, analytics are disabled by default: renderer analytics calls are inactive, and the main-process tracking gate is disabled. The retained telemetry setting does not currently enable event collection. Automatic crash upload initialization was not found in the inspected desktop source. This is separate from analytics; a setting labeled telemetry should not be interpreted as a crash-reporting control.

If you send a support report, logs, screenshots, and attachments are shared only through the channel you choose. Review and redact them first. Build configuration and later releases may change these behaviors; check the release notes.

This website

This static website contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external font requests, or forms. The hosting service may process ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser details to serve and secure the site. Downloads and support links take you to GitHub; its policies apply there.
